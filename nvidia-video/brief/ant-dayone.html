How we hold these profits to month end.

1
Stop-loss at break-even, straight away. A protective stop at the entry price means the position can no longer turn into a loss. Whatever the market does, the worst case is zero.
2
Trail the stop as ANT rises. Move the stop up behind the price, for example 8–10% below the highest close. New highs lock in more; a pull-back closes the trade in profit instead of giving it back.
3
Take part of the profit off the table. Close a third of the position now and a third at the next target. Realised profit cannot be lost; the rest stays in for the upside.
4
Hedge through the volatile days. New listings swing most in the first weeks. On event days a short CFD against part of the long position freezes the result until the day has passed, then the hedge is lifted.
5
Keep leverage and margin comfortable. At 1:10 a 10% move is 100% of margin. Keeping free margin high and position size disciplined is what lets the plan above run to 30 November without a forced close.
